# Privacy Policy

**Onlykinds Fitness** · Focuser LLC
Last updated September 1, 2026

## Overview

Onlykinds Fitness ("the app") is provided by Focuser LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we store the name and email address your provider shares with us, plus a provider-assigned identifier used to recognize your account.

**Training information.** The workouts you log — sets, reps, weights or times, notes, weigh-ins and bodyweight entries, and your progress through the programs. This is stored on your device, and when you are signed in it is synced to our cloud database so your progress follows you between devices.

**Membership information.** Subscriptions are purchased through Apple. We never see or store your payment card details; we receive your subscription status from Apple through RevenueCat, our subscription service provider, so the app knows which features are unlocked.

## How we use information

We use your information to run the app: saving and syncing your training history, keeping your programs and progress up to date, managing your membership, confirming account changes, and providing support.

We do not sell your information, and the app contains no third-party advertising.

## Sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the app: our cloud hosting provider for sign-in and syncing, RevenueCat for subscription status, and Apple and Google as sign-in providers. These providers process information on our behalf to deliver the service.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

## Retention and deletion

Your training information is kept for as long as your account exists so your history is available in the app. Signing out stops syncing, and removing the app from your device deletes anything stored only on that device.

To have your synced account and training data deleted, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be shown in the app with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy can be sent to info@focuserllc.com.
