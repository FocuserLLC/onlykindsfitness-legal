# Health, Fitness & Medical Disclaimer

**Onlykinds Fitness** · Focuser LLC
Last updated September 1, 2026

## Not medical advice

Onlykinds Fitness provides exercise instruction, demonstration videos, and tracking tools for general fitness purposes. Nothing in the app is medical advice, and no content creates a doctor–patient relationship. Always seek the advice of a physician or other qualified health provider with any questions regarding a medical condition.

## Get cleared first

Consult a physician and be cleared by a physician before beginning this or any exercise program. This is especially important if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, joint or back problems, are pregnant, are recovering from injury or illness, or take medication that affects exercise.

## Stop when something is wrong

Stop exercising immediately and seek medical help if you feel dizziness, chest pain, shortness of breath, nausea, or sharp pain. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

## Assumption of risk

Physical exercise carries an inherent risk of injury. By using the app you voluntarily assume that risk. You train at your own discretion and are responsible for choosing exercises, progressions, and intensities appropriate for your ability.
